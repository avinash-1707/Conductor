# Welcome to Conductor — Design Partner Onboarding

Conductor runs your content pipelines so they don't break, don't need
babysitting, and never lose work. A pipeline researches, pauses for your
reviewer's approval, writes, and delivers — and if anything fails mid-run, it
picks up from the last finished step instead of starting over (and instead of
re-spending your tokens).

As a design partner you use Conductor free. In exchange we ask for a short
weekly feedback call and honest notes when something feels wrong — the **Share
feedback** link in the sidebar reaches us directly.

## Your first run — about 10 minutes

1. **Create your account** and name your organization. Your org is your
   workspace: every run, approval, and setting lives inside it.
2. **Add your OpenRouter API key** (Settings → OpenRouter API key). Your
   pipelines run on your own key — we never spend tokens on your behalf. The
   key is stored encrypted and never shown again after you save it. Get one at
   openrouter.ai if you don't have one yet.
3. **Invite your reviewer** (Settings → Members & approvers). Anyone in your
   org can approve runs. They get an email with a join link — or copy the
   invite link from the pending invitation and send it yourself. You can skip
   this and approve your own runs while trying things out.
4. **Pick a template** (Workflows). Three are ready today:
   - **Blog Post Pipeline** — topic + keywords + tone → a finished draft.
   - **SEO Brief** — target keyword + audience → a complete content brief.
   - **Competitor Research** — your company + competitors → a comparison report.
5. **Launch.** You'll land on the run's live view: research streams in as it's
   written, then the run pauses and waits for your reviewer.
6. **Approve.** The reviewer opens Approvals, reads the research right on the
   card, and clicks Approve (or Reject — that ends the run cleanly). The run
   resumes within seconds, writes the draft live, and delivers it.

## What to expect day-to-day

- **The Runs page is home.** Anything needing attention — waiting on approval,
  or failed — sorts to the top. Statuses update live; no refreshing.
- **Every run keeps its full history**: each step's inputs, outputs, attempts,
  timing, and who approved what, when. Click any run to inspect it.
- **Failures are recoverable.** A failed run shows exactly which step failed
  and why, with a "Resume from last successful step" button — finished work
  (and spent tokens) are never repeated.
- **Approvals wait 24 hours** by default, then the run expires gracefully. A
  rejected or expired run is a decision, not an error.

## What we'd love feedback on

- Were the template parameters the right ones? What's missing?
- Was the research good enough to approve? Was the draft usable?
- Where should the output go? (Today runs deliver to a webhook/export — Notion,
  WordPress, and Webflow are on the list; your vote orders it.)
- Anything that confused you for more than ten seconds.
